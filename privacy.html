Privacy Policy

How Lilori handles information you share with us.

Updated September 14, 2026

What we collect

If you write to us or submit a form, we receive the details you include — typically your name, email, company, and the message itself. Newsletter signups collect an email address only.

How we use it

We use that information to reply, to understand whether we can help, and — if you opt in — to send an occasional email about search and content. We do not sell contact lists.

How long we keep it

Inquiry emails are kept as long as needed to do the work or as required by law. You can ask us to delete a record by writing to hello@lilori.com.
