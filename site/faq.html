FAQ

Questions we hear before kickoff.

If you are deciding between a rebuild, a content program, or just a technical SEO pass, start here.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are the foundation, but most clients also need content, social, and SEO so the site is actually found. We can do one piece or all three — we just won't pretend a pretty homepage is a marketing program.

How long does a typical site take?

Most full builds land in six to ten weeks from kickoff to a live, indexed site. Timelines slip when brand decisions aren't made, copy isn't ready, or a third-party integration shows up late. We flag those early.

Can you work with a site we already have?

Yes. Plenty of engagements start with a technical review, a content audit, and a punch list instead of a rebuild. If the current site is holding you back, we'll say so. If it isn't, we won't sell you a new one.

Do you take on retainers?

Yes — that's how SEO and marketing work compounds. We keep a small number of ongoing clients so the monthly report is a real conversation, not a PDF that nobody reads.

What does a free site review include?

We look at speed, structure, obvious SEO gaps, and whether the pages match how people actually search for you. You get a short, honest note on what to fix first — even if we aren't the right team to do it.

Where are you based?

We work remotely with businesses across industries. Kickoff and monthly reviews happen on a call; the rest happens in the work.
